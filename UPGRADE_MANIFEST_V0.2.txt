Scientific Discovery v0.2 upgrade overlay

Apply this archive at the root of the existing quatum_ai_research repository.
It intentionally does not include or delete the large research reports, audit
artifacts, or scientific_retrieval_ontology_v0_1 seed package already present.

- retrieval provider boundary, fixture provider, gateway client, retrieval runs
- document and mathematics representations
- problem families and extraction contracts
- structural similarity, embeddings interface, and clustering baseline
- cross-domain candidate ranking
- quantum algorithm/primitive/match representations
- AI algorithm-proposal contracts
- evaluation metrics and benchmark workflow

- expanded CLI, docs, tests, Makefile, smoke test
