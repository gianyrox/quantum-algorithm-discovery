# v0.10 Limits and Non-Claims

v0.10 implements the complete experimental machinery for pre-quantum structure discovery; it does not establish that the first baseline algorithms are scientifically optimal.

## Explicit non-claims

- The rule-based problem extractor is not a production scientific extractor.
- A shallow LaTeX AST is not a full mathematical parser or computer algebra system.
- Alpha-normalized resemblance is not mathematical equivalence.
- Structural similarity is not computational reducibility.
- A connected component is not automatically a valid scientific problem family.
- A cross-domain candidate is not proof of independent rediscovery.
- Low retrieval novelty is not completeness without coverage audits.
- Unknown vocabulary is not automatically a new scientific concept.
- Provider absence is not evidence that a work or concept does not exist.
- Preexisting quantum modules are outside the v0.10 active pipeline and must not bias corpus construction.

## What must be learned empirically

The next research phase must calibrate extraction quality, mathematical similarity, similarity weights, family thresholds, candidate ranking, and saturation thresholds against heterogeneous real corpora and human review. Every baseline is versioned so it can be replaced without invalidating prior runs.
